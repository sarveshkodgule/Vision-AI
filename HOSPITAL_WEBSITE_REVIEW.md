# Hospital website review and patient experience update

Reviewed 13 October 2026. This is a product review, not an accreditation or legal-compliance certification.

## Implemented in this update

- Results now lead with the saved model risk band, a plain-language explanation, model probability, the next action, hospital contact and urgent-symptom guidance. High screening risk is distinguished from diagnosed high myopia. No new predictions are generated by the UI.
- Screening reports have adjacent View PDF, Download and Email report actions in results and history. The viewer supports Escape, a close button and a download fallback. Email status distinguishes queued, sending, mail-server acceptance and failure. Sent reports cannot accidentally be resent from the toolbar.
- English/Marathi guidance preference persists between the patient portal and visit page. It covers the result summary, next steps, basic precautions, report controls/status, visit directions and interactive checklist. Existing form labels, detailed server-authored guidance and PDFs remain English; this is not a fully translated site.
- Public-listing photographs show the hospital entrance and consultation room on About. Source links are displayed. A confirmed reception photograph and an approved named doctor portrait remain to be supplied; the consultation photo is not labelled as a verified doctor portrait.
- Patient record, doctor-list, account, model, PDF and Google-login failures have visible feedback and retry paths. Loading history does not falsely appear as an empty record. Model submission errors preserve form entries.
- Email/password sign-in is available, with optional Google sign-in below it. Email-verified patient registration and password reset are restored. New users are patients; existing accounts retain their database identity and role when safely linked. Account email changes are blocked through the profile form. Setup: [GOOGLE_SIGN_IN_SETUP.md](GOOGLE_SIGN_IN_SETUP.md).

## Evidence and verification

- Final frontend ESLint and Vite production build passed. Both browser scripts passed; the patient flow exercised Low/Medium/High summaries, Marathi persistence, PDF preview/download/error recovery, email state and 320/390/1440px layouts. Google verification tests: 10 passed.
- The local default JWT signing secret was replaced with a randomly generated value without exposing it. Previously issued sessions need to sign in again. The supplied Google Client ID was copied from `.env.google.example` into the active `backend/.env`; the backend was restarted. Live configuration returns HTTP 200 and MongoDB is reachable. Google's button renders, but its SDK reports unauthorised origins for both `http://localhost:5173` and `http://127.0.0.1:5173`; update the Web client's authorised JavaScript origins before testing account sign-in.

- Google flow follows [Google Identity Services verification guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/gsi/web/guides/verify-google-id-token). Automated tests use synthetic claims/callbacks; real Google authentication remains dependent on the project's client ID and authorised origins.
- Medical explanations reference [NEI myopia guidance](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/nearsightedness-myopia) and [urgent retinal-detachment symptoms](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/retinal-detachment). A clinician should review Marathi patient copy before public clinical use.
- Photos come from the [hospital's Justdial listing](https://www.justdial.com/Aurangabad-Maharashtra/Sri-Sarveshwar-Netralaya-Near-Gurudwara-Jalna-Road-Aurangabad/9999PX240-X240-140912205949-U1R4_BZDET), at the user's request. Attribution is retained; no claim of an open licence or individual identity approval is made.
- Reusable browser checks: `Frontend/scripts/check-hospital-ui.cjs` and `Frontend/scripts/check-patient-experience.cjs`. API, Google and email calls in the latter are mocked; no real patient data or mail is sent.
- Full backend run: 57 checks, 54 passed; 3 ML checks failed because `backend/DL dataset/PALM/PALM/Testing/Images/T0001.jpg` and/or `ml/checkpoints/best_overall.pth` were unavailable. The Grad-CAM++ initialization test also reaches the missing-file guard before its mocked model loader. These failures were not hidden or converted into fake predictions.
